Isotopic Verification of Guimaras Mango Provenance

Dear Dr. Santos,

Thank you for forwarding the twenty-four fruit samples collected across the three candidate orchards. This letter confirms receipt and sets out the analytical approach we will follow.

We will determine stable carbon and nitrogen isotope ratios by isotope-ratio mass spectrometry, with elemental profiles by ED-XRF as a corroborating line of evidence. Provenance assignment will be reported with an explicit confidence interval; where the evidence does not separate two origins, we will say so rather than force a call.

A full Certificate of Analysis will follow within fifteen working days of sample preparation. Please let me know if the reporting format should align with a particular accreditation schedule.

Sincerely,
